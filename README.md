# Blueprint Swipe

A swipe-style app for discovering your dream home taste. Swipe through 100 photo cards covering architectural styles, roof forms, indoor-outdoor living, kitchens, bathrooms, wellness spaces, and more, then get a taste profile you can copy or download.

## How To Use

- Swipe right for **Yes**, left for **No**, and up for **Love** (or use the buttons or arrow keys).
- Progress is saved in your browser, so you can stop and come back any time.
- Open **Results** to see your profile, then use **Copy Results** or **Download JSON**.

The whole app is a single self-contained `index.html` (photos are embedded), and it works on phones, tablets, and desktop.
